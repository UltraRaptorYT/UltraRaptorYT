### a little about me

I'm **Soh Hong Yu**, a Computer Science student at the National University of Singapore, previously from Singapore Polytechnic's Applied AI & Analytics programme.

I like taking an idea from *“wouldn't it be funny if…”* to something people can actually use. That usually means building the whole thing: interfaces, APIs, automations, AI integrations, and whatever else gets it working.

Previously, I built cloud-compliance tooling at **GovTech Singapore**. I'm now working with **AI Singapore** on youth AI programmes and partnerships, and I still find myself building side projects and joining hackathons.
